Changelog

Dated entries showing what changed in each dashboard update. Newest first. Backed by the changelog array in data/metrics.json and git history at github.com/mb3700/fmb-ian-recovery.

May 23, 2026
Housing section: remove Lee County context block
  • Removed the Lee County context block below the housing card grid. Time-period mismatch (April Lee County data alongside March FMB cards) and mixed aggregation (single-month vs trailing-12-month) made it confusing.
  • Lee County DOM reference chips on the two DOM cards kept (single-number reference points are fine; can swap in March values if available later).
  • Housing section now consists of: title + provisional verification banner + 6-card grid.
May 23, 2026
Housing section: add "Provisional — pending MLS verification" banner
  • Added a yellow refresh-banner inside the Housing Market Activity section header (same visual pattern as the methodology page's bed-tax FY26 Q1 banner).
  • Banner states: all housing numbers will be confirmed against full Southwest Florida MLS data once received.
  • Parallel entry added to the "Pending Chamber Clarification" panel above so the verification-pending status is discoverable from the audit-trail page.
  • No card data changed — this is purely a transparency caveat. Any deltas surfaced by the MLS pull will be reconciled and documented in the Override Log when the verification data arrives.
May 23, 2026
Active Listings card simplified: drop days-of-supply / days-to-clear framing
  • Page now sticks to a single time concept — days on market — throughout the housing section. The supply-clearance framing (even using matching day units) was a distinct concept that risked confusing readers; dropped per Mark's call.
  • Active Listings card now has one Sanibel-style chip: "↓ High inventory — buyer's market".
  • Lee County context block trimmed to just closing counts + median + DOM. The "FMB's ~395 days to clear vs county-wide 131-176" comparison is gone.
  • data/metrics.json: removed currentDaysSupply / preIanDaysSupply fields from the Active Listings metric.
May 23, 2026
Housing cards: drop "Median Sale Price (All Types)" — confusing alongside SFR/condo split
  • Dropped the Median Sale Price (All Types) card. The All-Types DOM of 100 days read as a contradiction next to SFR 312 / Condo 94 (it's a valid weighted median — condos dominate volume — but it invited the wrong math at a glance).
  • Median price by type is preserved as a chip on each Sales card: SFR Sales shows $870K (was $1,080K · −19%); Condo Sales shows $472,500 (was $675K · −30%).
  • Active Listings supply chip relabeled from "~395 days of supply" to "~395 days to clear inventory at current sales pace" — keeps the day unit but distinguishes it from days-on-market.
  • Lee County context block uses the same phrasing (131 days SFR / 176 days condo to clear).
  • Housing grid is now 5 cards instead of 6.
May 23, 2026
Housing cards reframed: delta vs pre-Ian (negative when worse), red 'gap' progress bars
  • Headline framing flipped from "X% of March 2022" (reads positive) to "−X% vs March 2022" (reads as actual contraction). SFR sales now −53%, Condo sales −33%, Median Price (All) −28% — each with a red gap bar filling the loss.
  • Inverse-metric cards show absolute change instead of multiples: DOM SFR +290 days, DOM Condo +87 days, Active Listings +304% (+350 listings). Bars fill 100% red since current is multiples above pre-Ian.
  • Chips reframed with the same delta convention: median price −19% / −30%, sale-to-list −7.1 pts, above-list −30.8 pts.
  • Editorial rationale: "47% of pre-Ian" sounds like halfway-recovered when in fact the market contracted 53%. Negative deltas tell the truth.
May 23, 2026
Housing cards populated from Redfin direct API pull — Mar 2026 vs Mar 2022
  • Replaced 5 placeholder housing cards with populated data pulled directly from Redfin's Fort Myers Beach housing market chart API (region 6212).
  • SFR Sales 7 / 15 (47% of March 2022); Condo Sales 22 / 33 (67%); Median Price $542K / $750K (72%); SFR Days on Market 312 / 22 (14× longer); Condo Days on Market 94 / 7 (13× longer); Active Listings (All Types) 465 / 115 (4.0× pre-Ian — Redfin current snapshot vs a Southwest Florida MLS May 2022 report).
  • Methodology: same-month-prior-year (March 2026 vs March 2022). Pre-Ian baseline clean since Ian landfall was Sept 28, 2022. Single source on both sides — apples-to-apples comparison.
  • Sourcing switched from SWFL MLS via Barrera/Beach Talk Radio (annual context only) to Redfin direct (single-month splits by property type — IDs 6 = Single-Family, 3 = Condos/Co-ops, All = All Home Types).
  • Card lineup updated: Single-Family Home Sales / Condo Sales / Median Sale Price (All) / DOM — SFR / DOM — Condo. Replaces the earlier SFR Listings, Condo Listings, Vacant Lot Sales lineup (Redfin doesn't categorize lots, and pre-Ian active-listings split by type isn't on Redfin's API).
  • Optional future refinement: a direct Southwest Florida MLS pull could provide per-property-type splits for the listings card if a per-type breakout is wanted.
May 23, 2026
Housing Market section rebuilt — Sanibel-mirror format with SWFL MLS sourcing
  • Replaced four placeholder cards + Direction & Context cards with a 5-card grid mirroring the format used on the Sanibel recovery dashboard (sanibel-solutions.com): Residential Home Sales, Residential Listings, Condo Sales, Condo Listings, Vacant Lot Sales.
  • Methodology: most-recent-month vs same-month-2022 (rolling). Any month before October 2022 is a clean pre-Ian baseline since Ian made landfall September 28, 2022. Same-month comparison preserves seasonality.
  • Data sourcing: Southwest Florida MLS data published in Beach Talk Radio News. Full-year context populated (SFR 2021=192, 2024=70; condo 2021=401, 2024=166; lots 2024=32). Single-month current and pre-Ian figures pending a direct MLS pull via FMB Chamber.
  • Active-listings cards populated current-side from most recent published count (201 SFR / 316 condo, May 2025); pre-Ian baselines pending.
  • Added Lee County context block below the grid: April 2026 Florida Realtors / RPCRA aggregates (SFR 313 closings at $465K, Condo 297 closings at $246.5K) and FMB zip totals from Realtytrac ($614.2K median sold, 526 TTM closings, 667 listed).
  • Dropped the Home Sales YoY card (Feb 2026 +69%) and standalone Median Price + DOM card — replaced by the new sales cards and Lee County context block.
  • Tightened the Chamber outreach draft to ask for single-month figures specifically.
May 23, 2026
Consistency audit + May 2026 Update rebrand

  • VR rooms note: added 3-bucket reconciliation (operating + permanently lost + rebuilding partial).
  • Rebrand: "Q1 2026 Baseline" → "May 2026 Update" across the dashboard.
