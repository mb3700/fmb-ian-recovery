Changelog

Dated entries showing what changed in each dashboard update. Newest first. Backed by the changelog array in data/metrics.json and git history at github.com/mb3700/fmb-ian-recovery.

May 23, 2026
Housing Market section rebuilt — Sanibel-mirror format with SWFL MLS sourcing
  • Replaced four placeholder cards + Direction & Context cards with a 5-card grid mirroring the format used on the Sanibel recovery dashboard (sanibel-solutions.com): Residential Home Sales, Residential Listings, Condo Sales, Condo Listings, Vacant Lot Sales.
  • Methodology: most-recent-month vs same-month-2022 (rolling). Any month before October 2022 is a clean pre-Ian baseline since Ian made landfall September 28, 2022. Same-month comparison preserves seasonality.
  • Data sourcing: Southwest Florida MLS via Jorge Barrera (Premiere Plus Realty), published in Beach Talk Radio News. Full-year context populated (SFR 2021=192, 2024=70; condo 2021=401, 2024=166; lots 2024=32). Single-month current and pre-Ian figures pending Barrera/RPCRA pull via FMB Chamber.
  • Active-listings cards populated current-side from most recent published count (201 SFR / 316 condo, May 2025); pre-Ian baselines pending.
  • Added Lee County context block below the grid: April 2026 Florida Realtors / RPCRA aggregates (SFR 313 closings at $465K, Condo 297 closings at $246.5K) and FMB zip totals from Realtytrac ($614.2K median sold, 526 TTM closings, 667 listed).
  • Dropped the Home Sales YoY card (Feb 2026 +69%) and standalone Median Price + DOM card — replaced by the new sales cards and Lee County context block.
  • Tightened the Chamber outreach draft to ask for single-month figures specifically; added Jorge Barrera as an alternate channel (most direct path to MLS pulls he already compiles).
May 23, 2026
